# Same-office county comparison: 2022 vs 2026 NC Senate

The Election Night North Carolina county drawer now includes **two distinct historical reference points**, but only after the actual 2026 NCSBE ZIP has been published, source fingerprint and contest verified, and its 2026 Senate county counts approved:

- Full 2022 North Carolina **Senate** D/R margin (same office; different nominees, environment, turnout, and timing).
- Full 2024 North Carolina **presidential** D/R margin (different office as well as different electorate and candidates).

Both are compared strictly by the same county FIPS with the presently **reported** 2026 Senate D/R candidate vote margin. The 2022 ZIP is SHA-pinned and replayed against every website county row in the parent source PR. Each comparison has its own audited historical source, provenance checks, and source link. The 2022 baseline is **not the same thing as estimated final ballots**.

The 2026 source configuration stays disabled. Browser tests cover missing data, invalid ZIP SHA, stale results, approval revocation and restored valid data at desktop and iPhone-size widths. A failed historical data fetch cannot turn its baseline into live results.

**No geographic needle or chamber-forecast promotion:** partial county results may reflect which vote methods or precincts posted first. The observed margin difference from even the same-office 2022 totals is not a statistically calibrated forecast of the rest of that county, North Carolina, or the nation.

Next: verify official 2026 reporting completeness or estimated ballots remaining and calibrate county/method reporting-order bias on multiple historical elections. Do not simply extrapolate the 2022 margin difference.
