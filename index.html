What exactly is being estimated?

Two separate figures: the 0–100 score is an operational monitoring priority. The percentage is our rough, subjective probability that the named incident prevents at least 100 otherwise-eligible people from casting a countable ballot after ordinary remedies and review. Neither measures election fraud or who wins.

The probabilities are judgmental working estimates with deliberately broad ranges: registration backlog 12% (3–30%), ten-county Sunday-hours change 10% (2–25%), voter-list litigation without a demonstrated access effect 1% (0.1–5%). They are not historically calibrated. The records/county counts show the scale of the issue, not how many ballots will be lost. Each figure will be revised when processing reports, alternative access and actual voting data arrive. Full assumptions ↗
