What do the four endings mean?

Blue Wave: D wins BOTH chambers (≥218 House, ≥51 Democratic-aligned Senate). Blue Tsunami: ≥235 House and ≥52 Senate. Shrug: split control. Red Dawn: Republicans hold both. These categories are exclusive; stronger Tsunami draws are excluded from Wave.

🧪 Electorate Lab · What if the voter coalitions shift? Real JackStradamus model · hypothetical state shifts

Try a different electorate for one state. Specify the share of two-party voters in each group and how many points its Democratic-minus-Republican margin changes. The shares are your assumptions, not measured Census or turnout percentages. Groups must not overlap. Only that state's race priors change; the shared national model, candidates and covariance remain unchanged.

Select voter-share assumptions and run a conditional scenario.

This uses the real House + Senate + governor correlated simulation. No scenario changes the headline odds, actual election returns or saved model.

Math: state margin shift = Σ (cohort's assumed fraction of two-party voters × D−R margin change). Fixed cohort turnout shares are assumed. This is a local sensitivity, not a prediction of actual demographic behavior, polling accuracy, precinct reporting or an Election Night needle.

What exactly is being estimated?

Two separate figures: the 0–100 score is an operational monitoring priority. The percentage is our rough, subjective probability that the named incident prevents at least 100 otherwise-eligible people from casting a countable ballot after ordinary remedies and review. Neither measures election fraud or who wins.

The probabilities are judgmental working estimates with deliberately broad ranges: registration backlog 12% (3–30%), ten-county Sunday-hours change 10% (2–25%), voter-list litigation without a demonstrated access effect 1% (0.1–5%). They are not historically calibrated. The records/county counts show the scale of the issue, not how many ballots will be lost. Each figure will be revised when processing reports, alternative access and actual voting data arrive. Full assumptions ↗
