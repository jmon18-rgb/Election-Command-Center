How it works
Poll independenceOne latest observation per pollster and named-candidate matchup.
RecencyExponential decay; half-life adjustable.
Sample sizeSquare-root weighting; missing sizes receive a stated default.
SponsorshipExplicitly partisan-sponsored polls excluded.
UncertaintyCalibration and backtesting required before publishing statistical confidence intervals.
