root (and any county IDs supplied), nonnegative safe integers and county sums
equal to statewide totals for every candidate. Constitution and Libertarian
counts remain Other; new write-in rows require review. Historical Conservative
and write-in counts remain separately recorded in the original2020 baseline.

Test/mock/disputed/disabled feeds, unreviewed rounds, missing/stale/future clocks
and positive pre-close reports are excluded. All winner flags, called_at and
percent-reporting fields are ignored by this counts-only connection. No source
winner is promoted into a call or a celebration. Zero is not estimated completion.
